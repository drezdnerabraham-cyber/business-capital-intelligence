About BackStone Capital Connect

Working capital for United States small businesses, underwritten on revenue.

Who we are

BackStone Capital Connect is a business funding service operated by AFE International LLC, a limited liability company based in Brooklyn, New York. We help small and mid-sized United States businesses access working capital from $5,000 to $5,000,000 through revenue-based financing, including merchant cash advances and related commercial funding products.

What we do

Small business owners are routinely turned away by traditional lenders for reasons that have little to do with whether their business is healthy — a thin credit file, a short operating history, an industry a bank does not like, or simply a timeline that does not fit a 60-day underwriting process.

We take a different starting point. We look at the revenue moving through your business. If your deposits are consistent and your business is generating money, there is usually a funding structure that works — and we can often return an approval decision in as little as 24 hours.

Our team collects your application and recent business bank statements, reviews your file, and works with our network of capital providers to put real options in front of you. A funding specialist walks you through the amount, the total cost and the remittance schedule in plain language before you commit to anything. There is no cost to apply and no obligation to accept an offer.

How businesses use the funding

Our clients use capital for payroll, inventory purchases, equipment, expansion and build-outs, marketing, tax obligations, bridging receivables and covering seasonal slowdowns. We work with businesses across restaurants and food service, retail, construction and the trades, trucking and logistics, auto repair, medical and dental practices, salons and spas, staffing, e-commerce and professional services.

How we work with you

  • Straight answers. We tell you the total cost and the payment structure up front, before you sign.
  • One point of contact. You work with a specialist who knows your file, not a queue.
  • Business purposes only. Every product we arrange is for commercial use. A merchant cash advance is a purchase of future receivables, not a loan, and we will explain exactly what that means for your business.
  • Your choice on contact. Agreeing to receive calls or text messages from us is never a condition of applying for or receiving funding.
