1. Who we are

BackStone Capital Connect ("BackStone Capital Connect," "we," "us" or "our") is a business funding service operated by AFE International LLC, a limited liability company located at 1150 45th St, Brooklyn, NY 11219, United States.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, submit a funding application, call us or exchange text messages with us; how we use that information; and the choices available to you. By using our website or services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information we collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, information collected automatically when you use our website, and information we receive from third parties in connection with a funding application.

Information you provide

  • Contact information — your name, business name, mobile and business telephone numbers, email address and mailing address.
  • Business information — industry, time in business, entity type, monthly revenue, business address, and federal tax identification number where required.
  • Financial information — business bank statements, deposit history, processing statements, existing financing obligations and related documents you submit for underwriting.
  • Identification information — owner name, date of birth, government identification and, where required for underwriting, Social Security number.
  • Communications — the content of emails, text messages, contact forms and call notes exchanged with us.

Information collected automatically

  • IP address, browser type, device type, operating system and language settings.
  • Pages viewed, referring page, links clicked and the dates and times of your visits.
  • Information collected through cookies and similar technologies, as described in Section 6.

Information from third parties

  • Consumer and commercial credit reporting agencies, where you have authorized us to obtain a report.
  • Business verification, fraud prevention and public records databases.
  • Bank data verification providers you connect when submitting an application.
  • Referral partners, marketing partners and independent sales organizations that submit your inquiry to us with your permission.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Receive, evaluate and underwrite your funding application.
  • Verify your identity, your business and the accuracy of the information you provide.
  • Present funding options, prepare offers and complete funding transactions.
  • Communicate with you about your application, your account and your servicing obligations by phone, email and text message.
  • Provide customer support and respond to your questions and requests.
  • Send you information about products and services that may be relevant to your business, where permitted by law and consistent with your consent choices.
  • Detect, investigate and prevent fraud, unauthorized activity and other misuse of our services.
  • Operate, maintain, secure and improve our website and services.
  • Comply with applicable laws, regulations, subpoenas, audits and lawful requests from government authorities.

4. SMS / text messaging policy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories of information sharing described in this Privacy Policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties or affiliates.

4.1 Program description and consent

BackStone Capital Connect operates an SMS/text messaging program for United States business customers and applicants. We send text messages only to mobile numbers whose owners have given prior express consent — for example, by entering a mobile number on our online funding application, by providing a mobile number to a funding specialist over the phone, by submitting a contact form on this website, or by texting us first.

Consent to receive text messages from BackStone Capital Connect is not a condition of any purchase, of applying for funding, or of receiving funding. You may apply for and receive funding without agreeing to receive text messages.

4.2 Types of messages we send

  • Application updates — confirmations that we received your application, requests for missing documents, and status notifications.
  • Funding offers — approval notifications, offer terms and requests to review or sign documents.
  • Customer care and follow-ups — replies to your questions, appointment and callback confirmations, servicing reminders and follow-up messages regarding an open inquiry.

4.3 Message frequency

Message frequency varies. The number of messages you receive depends on your interaction with us and the status of your application or account. You will typically receive messages only in response to a request you made or in connection with an active application.

4.4 Cost

Message and data rates may apply. BackStone Capital Connect does not charge you for text messages, but your mobile carrier's standard message and data rates apply to every message sent to or received from us. Contact your wireless provider for details about your plan.

4.5 How to opt out (STOP)

You can cancel the SMS service at any time. Reply STOP to any text message you receive from us — or text STOP to the number that messaged you. After you send STOP, we will send you a single message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and you will no longer receive text messages from us. You may also opt out by calling us at (516) 774-3423 or (347) 469-4652.

If you want to rejoin the program after opting out, sign up again the same way you did originally, or text START to the number you were messaging with, and we will resume sending messages to you.

4.6 How to get help (HELP)

If you are experiencing problems with the messaging program or need assistance, reply HELP to any message for help, or contact us directly at (516) 774-3423 or (347) 469-4652. Replying HELP returns our contact information and support instructions.

4.7 Supported carriers

Our messaging program is supported by major United States wireless carriers, including but not limited to AT&T, Verizon Wireless, T-Mobile, Sprint, U.S. Cellular, Boost Mobile, Cricket Wireless, MetroPCS, Google Voice, Virgin Mobile, and their affiliated and reseller carriers.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message delivery is subject to effective transmission by your wireless carrier and is outside of our control.

4.8 Mobile information sharing

We do not sell, rent, lease or otherwise share mobile telephone numbers, text messaging opt-in data or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes, and we do not include this information in any list that is sold or licensed. Mobile information may only be shared with vendors that act on our behalf and strictly for the purpose of delivering messages you have consented to receive — for example, our SMS platform provider and telecommunications carriers — and those vendors are contractually prohibited from using the information for any other purpose.

4.9 Privacy of your messages

Text messages you exchange with us are stored in our customer records and treated the same way as other information you provide to us under this Privacy Policy. Please do not send full Social Security numbers, bank account numbers or other highly sensitive information by text message.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. Subject at all times to the mobile information restriction in Section 4, we may share information in the following circumstances:

  • Funding partners and underwriters — with capital providers, funders, lenders and underwriting partners for the purpose of evaluating, approving and funding your application.
  • Service providers — with vendors that perform services on our behalf, such as document processing, e-signature, bank verification, hosting, customer relationship management, analytics and communications delivery. These vendors are permitted to use your information only to provide services to us.
  • Verification and fraud prevention — with credit bureaus, identity verification and fraud prevention services, where authorized.
  • Professional advisors — with our attorneys, accountants, auditors and insurers.
  • Legal and regulatory — when required by law, regulation, court order, subpoena or governmental request, or where we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety or the safety of others, or to investigate fraud.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, sale of assets, reorganization or similar transaction, subject to the mobile information restriction above.
  • With your direction — with any other party when you ask us to do so or otherwise consent.

6. Cookies and tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site functioning, remember your preferences and understand how visitors use our pages. You can set your browser to refuse cookies or to alert you when cookies are being sent; some parts of the site may not function properly if you do.

Some browsers transmit a "Do Not Track" signal. Because there is no common industry standard for interpreting these signals, our website does not currently respond to them.

7. Data security

We maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect the information we collect against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration and destruction. These include encrypted transmission of information submitted through our website, restricted internal access on a need-to-know basis, and vendor diligence requirements. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data retention

We retain your information for as long as needed to provide our services, to maintain business and financial records, to resolve disputes, to enforce our agreements, and to comply with our legal, tax, audit and regulatory obligations. Records related to funding transactions are generally retained for the periods required by applicable financial recordkeeping laws. When information is no longer required, we delete it or de-identify it.

Records of SMS opt-outs are retained so that we can continue to honor your opt-out request.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to our legal and recordkeeping obligations.
  • Opt out of marketing communications, including email and text messages.
  • Be free from discrimination for exercising any of these rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 13. We may need to verify your identity before responding. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time by using the unsubscribe link in the email, and from text messages by replying STOP.

10. Children's privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and for individuals who are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Third-party links

Our website may link to third-party websites, including our online application portal and partner sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of every site you visit.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be highlighted on this page. Your continued use of our website or services after an update means you accept the revised policy.
