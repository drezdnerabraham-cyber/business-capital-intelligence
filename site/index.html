How it works

Three steps from application to funded. Most businesses complete the first step in under ten minutes.

Apply online

Complete the short online application with your business details. There is no cost to apply and no obligation to accept an offer.

Get reviewed & approved

Send your three most recent business bank statements. A specialist reviews your revenue and returns a decision — often within 24 hours.

Review terms & get funded

We walk you through the amount, the total cost and the remittance schedule. Sign electronically and funds can be deposited to your business account, frequently within one business day.

Who we fund

BackStone Capital Connect works with established, revenue-generating United States businesses across a wide range of industries — including restaurants and food service, retail, construction and trades, trucking and logistics, auto repair, medical and dental practices, salons and spas, staffing firms, e-commerce and professional services.

Businesses that qualify most easily typically have been operating for at least six months, maintain consistent monthly deposits into a business bank account, and can provide recent business bank statements. If you are not sure whether you qualify, apply or call us — a review costs nothing.
