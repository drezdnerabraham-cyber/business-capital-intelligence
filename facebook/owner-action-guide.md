Everything that requires YOU personally. Nothing else.

## Step 1 — Create the Facebook Page (15 min)

1. Go to **facebook.com/pages/create**
2. Select category: **Financial Services → Lending**
3. Page name: **Backstone Capital Connect**
4. Complete the About section with this text (copy-paste exactly):

> "Backstone Capital Connect helps small business owners explore working-capital options. We work with restaurants, contractors, retail businesses, trucking companies, and more. No pressure, no obligation — just honest information and access to funding options that fit your situation. Apply online or message us with any questions."

5. Set the CTA button: **"Apply Now"** or **"Contact Us"** → link to your application URL
6. Add UTM tracking to your CTA link:
   ```
   https://[your-application-url]?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=content_engine
   ```

Every link you include in posts should use:

## Questions / Blockers

If you hit any of these, here's the answer:

**"I don't have a Fliki account"** — Log into fliki.ai with the same Google account you use for Make.com. Your renders should be there under your account.

**"I can't find the Canva designs"** — Use the edit URLs in `canva-design-assets.md`. You'll be prompted to copy the design into your account before editing.

**"I don't have a Meta Business Suite account"** — Go to business.facebook.com and set one up with your personal Facebook login. It's free.

**"What should the application URL be?"** — If you don't have a dedicated application page yet, use your main website URL for now, then update the CTA later.
