# Backstone Capital Connect — Facebook Content Engine

## Brand Foundation

**Business:** Backstone Capital Connect  
**Niche:** Merchant Cash Advance / Alternative Business Funding  
**Audience:** Small business owners — restaurants, contractors, retail, trucking, healthcare — who need working capital and either (a) don't qualify for traditional bank financing or (b) need capital faster than a bank can provide it.

### Brand Voice
- Professional, direct, plain-English
- Knowledgeable without being condescending
- Honest about what MCA is and isn't
- Zero fluff, zero fake urgency, zero fake social proof
- Treats the merchant like an intelligent adult

### What We NEVER Claim
- Guaranteed approvals
- Guaranteed funding amounts
- Guaranteed rates or terms
- "Lowest rates" (we don't know this)
- Fake customer testimonials
- Fabricated case studies
- Misleading factor-rate-as-APR claims
- We've been in business X years (unless verified)
- Any claim under FTC MCA enforcement scrutiny (speed/amount/approval odds)

### Visual Brand Direction
- **Primary:** Deep navy (#0A1628)
- **Accent:** Gold/amber (#C8963E)
- **Background:** Clean white (#FFFFFF) or light gray (#F7F8FA)
- **Typography:** Clean sans-serif; bold headlines, readable body
- **Style:** Modern business-finance — NOT stock-photo cheesy, NOT flashy crypto/fintech
- **No:** stock photos of people counting cash, fake "funded" screenshots, lifestyle imagery

## Content Strategy

### Mix Targets
- **60–70% Education/Value** — teaches merchants something genuinely useful
- **20–30% Trust/Authority** — shows how Backstone works, FAQs, process transparency
- **10–20% Offer/CTA** — invites qualified merchants to explore options

### Core Content Pillars

**Pillar 1 — Business Finance Basics**
Cash flow fundamentals, working capital concepts, how different financing products work.
Goal: be the page a merchant bookmarks when they want to understand business finance.

**Pillar 2 — The MCA Education Layer**
What MCA actually is, how factor rates work, when it makes sense vs. when it doesn't.
Goal: merchants who work with us are informed — this reduces friction AND positions us as trustworthy.

**Pillar 3 — Funding Readiness**
What funders look at, how to improve fundability, what bank statements signal, common reasons advances are declined.
Goal: help merchants prepare — this also qualifies inbound leads.

**Pillar 4 — The Backstone Process**
How we work, what to expect, what makes a strong application, what happens after submission.
Goal: reduce friction and anxiety in the decision to apply.

**Pillar 5 — Industry Situations (Vertical-Specific)**
Restaurants, contractors, trucking, retail, healthcare — each vertical has specific cash-flow patterns. Address each one directly.
Goal: merchants recognize their own situation and feel understood.

**Pillar 6 — Soft CTA**
Non-pushy, low-friction invitations to start a conversation or check eligibility.
Goal: generate qualified traffic to the application without feeling like an ad.

## Post Format Library

### Format A — Text + Branded Graphic (Static Image)
Best for: quick insights, stats, quotes, reminders.
Specs: 1080×1080px or 1080×1350px (portrait preferred for feed).

### Format B — Carousel (Multi-Image)
Best for: step-by-step processes, "X things you need to know," myth vs. fact comparisons.
Specs: 2–7 slides, each 1080×1080px.

### Format C — Infographic (Single Static)
Best for: comparisons (MCA vs. bank loan), timelines, checklists.
Specs: 1080×1920px (tall) or 1080×1350px.

### Format D — Text Post (No Image)
Best for: short, punchy insights that don't need visual support.
Use sparingly — 2–3x per month max.

### Format E — Animated Text Video / Reel (Faceless)
Best for: "did you know" style short-form video, key-stat animation, process explainer.
Specs: 1080×1920px vertical, 15–60 seconds, captions required.
Tools: Canva animated graphic → video export, or Descript for narrated explainers with ElevenLabs TTS.

### Format F — AI Voiceover Explainer (Faceless Video)
Best for: 60–90 second educational explainers on a specific topic.
Tools: ElevenLabs TTS (via Make.com existing pipeline) + B-roll stock video + captions.
No face, no recording required.
