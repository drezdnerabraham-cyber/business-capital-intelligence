About AFE International

AFE International is an independent business funding broker connecting revenue-generating businesses with vetted funding providers. We simplify the capital connection process so business owners can focus on growth.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does AFE International lend money directly? +
No. AFE International is a broker, not a lender. We do not lend directly, underwrite loans, or make credit decisions. All funding is provided by third-party funding specialists in our network. We connect you with the provider that best fits your business needs.
What types of businesses do you work with? +
We work with businesses across the United States in a wide range of industries. Whether you run a restaurant, retail store, medical practice, trucking company, or any other type of business, we can help match you with the right funding solution based on your revenue and goals.
How fast can I get funded? +
Once matched with a provider and approved, funding can be available within 24-48 hours depending on the funding product and provider. The entire process from first contact to funding can move very quickly.
Is there a cost for your service? +
Our consultation is free. We earn a referral fee from the funding providers in our network—you do not pay us directly. The terms and costs of any funding product will be clearly disclosed by the provider before you commit.
What do I need to qualify? +
Requirements vary by provider and product, but generally you'll need to demonstrate consistent monthly revenue and have been in business for a minimum period. We evaluate your profile and match you with providers whose criteria you're most likely to meet.
How do I opt out of SMS messages? +
You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any text message you receive from us. We will confirm your removal and you will no longer receive SMS messages. You can also reply HELP for assistance or call us at (347) 469-4652.
