SMS Usage Policy

AFE International's A2P SMS communications are used exclusively for:

  • ✓ Business-to-business loan application updates and status notifications
  • ✓ Verification of applicant business information
  • ✓ Funder matching notifications and funding opportunity alerts
  • ✓ Payment and disbursement confirmations
  • ✓ Required documentation requests
  • ✓ Appointment confirmations and follow-ups
  • ✓ Authorized service communications from our verified platform

No promotional messages, no unsolicited marketing, no spam. All SMS communications are sent to users who have opted in and consented to receive business notifications from AFE International.
